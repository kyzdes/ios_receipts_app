# Recipe Manager v2.0 - Product Roadmap

## Vision
Transform Recipe Manager from a simple recipe storage app into an **intelligent cooking companion** that makes cooking easier, more fun, and social. Version 2.0 will leverage AI, gamification, and community features to create an addictive cooking experience.

---

## 🎯 Core Goals

1. **Make cooking effortless** - AI-powered assistance, smart timers, voice guidance
2. **Build a community** - Social features, sharing, collaborative cooking
3. **Gamify the experience** - Achievements, streaks, challenges, leaderboards
4. **Personalize everything** - AI recommendations, dietary preferences, taste learning
5. **Plan ahead** - Meal planning, shopping lists, inventory tracking
6. **Track health** - Nutritional analysis, calorie tracking, macro goals

---

## 🎨 Design Principles

1. **Simplicity First** - Don't overwhelm with features
2. **Mobile-First** - Most cooking happens in kitchen with phone
3. **Hands-Free Priority** - Enable cooking without touching device
4. **Visual Appeal** - Food photography is key
5. **Fast & Responsive** - No lag during cooking
6. **Accessible** - Voice, large text, high contrast
7. **Fun & Delightful** - Animations, celebrations, surprises
